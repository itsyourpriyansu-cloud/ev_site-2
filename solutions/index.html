FAQCommon questions.

Still have questions?

Tell us about your site and our team will get back to you with clear next steps.

01Do you only supply chargers, or install them too?

Both. We supply chargers alone, supply and install, or deliver the whole site turnkey: feasibility, design, procurement, civil and electrical works, commissioning and handover.

02What happens during a site assessment?

We check demand, site access and power availability, the parking layout, cable routes and the civil and electrical scope, then recommend chargers and a phased plan.

03Who handles DISCOM approvals and transformer upgrades?

We include them in the survey and list them separately in the quote, so you can see what the chargers cost and what the site needs.

04Can we start small and add capacity later?

Yes. NEO chargers take extra power modules in the field, and we design the electrical scope for expansion, so growth does not mean starting over.

05What support do you provide after launch?

Remote monitoring, preventive maintenance, troubleshooting and 24/7 support, with AMC and managed-operations options agreed in the contract.
