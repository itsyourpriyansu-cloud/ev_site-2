03

Standards-based hardware

Open protocols. No lock-in.

Our chargers are specified to connect over OCPP, the open protocol most charge-point management platforms use, so you can choose or change your software later without replacing hardware.

  • OCPP 1.6J backend connectivity, confirmed per model
  • Works with Envizon or third-party management software
  • CCS2 connector to IEC 62196-3
  • Session and status data available to your operator
  • Hardware documentation shared at handover
04

  • One contact for hardware and service questions

The questions site owners, societies and fleet managers ask us most before a project starts.

01What does Envizon EV offer?

We help businesses, residential communities and fleets plan, supply, install and commission EV charging, from the first site assessment to a working charger.

02Which charger power is right for my site?

It depends on how long vehicles stay, how many charge at once and the power available at your site. We recommend a configuration after a site assessment.

03Do you handle site assessment and installation?

Yes. We assess power availability and parking layout, then plan, install and commission the chargers with a documented handover.

04Which vehicles and connectors are supported?

Our DC fast chargers are specified for the CCS2 connector used by most new electric cars in India. Exact compatibility is confirmed per model.

05How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on site readiness, electrical approvals and charger availability. We share a project plan after the site assessment.

06Do you offer partnership programmes?

Yes. We work with site hosts and business partners. Share your details and our team will walk you through the available options.
