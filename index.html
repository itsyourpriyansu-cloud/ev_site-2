01What does Envizon EV offer?

Four connected services: NEO Smart Series DC chargers, installation and commissioning, CMS, apps and payments, and ongoing monitoring and maintenance, for businesses, fleets, charging operators and infrastructure developers.

02Which charger power is right for my site?

It depends on how long vehicles stay, how many charge at once and the power available at your site. We recommend a configuration after a site assessment.

03Do you handle site assessment and installation?

Yes. We assess power availability and parking layout, then plan, install and commission the chargers with a documented handover.

04Which vehicles and connectors are supported?

The NEO Smart Series uses the CCS2 (Combo-2) connector found on most new electric cars in India, from 150 to 1000 V DC. Compatibility for buses and commercial vehicles is confirmed per model.

05How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on site readiness, electrical approvals and charger availability. We share a project plan after the site assessment.

06Do you offer partnership programmes?

Yes. We work with manufacturers, technology partners, dealers, distributors and regional partners. Share your details and our team will walk you through the options.

07What support do you provide after installation?

Preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, and technical and customer support, with 24/7 availability for operational and service requests.
