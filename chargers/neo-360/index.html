OverviewThe flagship: 360 kW and 500 A boost for high-capacity hubs and corridors.

One floor-standing cabinet, 1100 × 2000 × 850 mm, with everything a driver and an operator need on the front panel.

  1. 01

    10.1-inch touch display

    LCD touch panel showing both guns side by side. The touchscreen is rated IK08.

  2. 02

    RFID, QR and app start

    Tap an RFID card (ISO/IEC 14443 A/B, FeliCa, MIFARE), scan the panel QR code or use the app with OTP. Auto Charge starts registered vehicles on plug-in.

  3. 03

    Emergency stop

    One emergency stop button on the front panel, backed by nine electrical protections.

  4. 04

    Two CCS2 guns

    CCS2 (Combo-2) at 150–1000 V DC, 375 A (boost up to 500 A) per gun, each output isolated from the other.

  5. 05

    5.5 m charging cables

    Usable cable length on each gun, so one cabinet serves two bays.

  6. 06

    Outdoor enclosure

    GI 120 GSM body with a fire-resistant polyester coat. IP55, IK10, rated −30 °C to +55 °C.

FAQAbout the NEO Smart Series 360 kW.

Still have questions?

Tell us about your site and our team will get back to you with clear next steps.

01What does the 500 A boost do?

Each gun is rated at 375 A and can boost up to 500 A for vehicles that accept higher current, which shortens charging stops.

02What supply does it need?

Up to 405 kVA at 415 V AC, 3-phase. Most sites need a dedicated transformer at this power, which the site assessment scopes.

03Which vehicles can it charge?

Vehicles with a CCS2 (Combo-2) inlet, which most new electric cars in India use, from 150 to 1000 V DC. Compatibility for buses and commercial vehicles is confirmed per model.
