FAQAbout the Atom AC Wallbox.

Still have questions?

Tell us about your site and our team will get back to you with clear next steps.

01Will a 22 kW wallbox charge my car at 22 kW?

Only if the car's on-board charger and your supply support three-phase 22 kW. Many cars accept 7.4 or 11 kW on AC, so we size the wallbox to the vehicle and the connection.

02Can residents in a society share wallboxes?

Yes. RFID cards or the app identify each user, so shared points can be used by registered residents only.

03Does it need an internet connection?

No. The wallbox is controlled over Bluetooth from the app, and RFID works locally.
