01 Residential & apartments

Charging at home, without the guesswork.

Individual homes need a safe, right-sized point. Societies need shared charging that's fair to residents and kind to the building's supply. We plan both around your connection and parking.

What we cover

  • Load check on the home or society supply
  • Wallbox or smart-socket recommendation per bay
  • RFID or app access per resident
  • Scheduled charging for off-peak tariffs
  • Installation and handover to the society

Recommended chargers

  • Atom AC Smart Socket
  • Atom AC Wallbox

02 Retail, hospitality & workplaces

Charging your guests and staff will use.

Hotels, malls, offices and shops want charging that matches how long people stay. We size AC for long dwell times and add DC where turnover is quick, with payments built in.

What we cover

  • Dwell-time and footfall-based sizing
  • Single- or dual-gun AC for long-stay bays
  • DC for quick-turnover locations
  • UPI and card payments for paid charging
  • Signage and user workflow at launch

Recommended chargers

  • Atom AC Smart Socket
  • Atom AC Destination
  • Atom DC Fast Charger

03 Fleets & logistics

Depot charging sized to your duty cycle.

Fleets run on schedules, not on chance. We plan depot power around shift patterns and route lengths, so vehicles leave charged and the connection isn't overloaded.

What we cover

  • Duty-cycle and shift-pattern analysis
  • Overnight AC vs opportunity DC charging mix
  • Depot load planning and phasing
  • RFID per driver or vehicle
  • Maintenance and uptime reporting

Recommended chargers

  • Atom DC Fast Charger
  • Atom AC Destination

04 Charge Point Operators

Hardware that fits your network.

Operators need chargers that join their backend and stay online. We confirm OCPP and payment compatibility per model before you order, then deliver and support each site.

What we cover

  • OCPP 1.6J options on networked models
  • Backend compatibility confirmed before order
  • Multi-site rollout and commissioning
  • Payments, RFID and app access
  • Fault triage and service escalation

Recommended chargers

  • Atom DC Fast Charger
  • Atom AC Destination

05 Bus depots

High-power charging for electric buses.

A bus depot is a small power plant. We plan high-power DC around the depot's timetable, the transformer and DISCOM approvals, and the space between bays.

What we cover

  • 180–360 kW DC charging
  • Transformer and DISCOM scope in the survey
  • Gun scheduling around the timetable
  • Layout for bus manoeuvring and cable reach
  • Preventive maintenance plans

Recommended chargers

  • Atom DC Fast Charger

06 Public & highway charging

Fast charging where drivers stop.

Fuel stations, highway restaurants, malls and metro parking need quick, reliable public charging. We plan DC for turnover and add payments drivers already use.

What we cover

  • 30–360 kW DC for short stops
  • Site selection and power check
  • UPI and card payments
  • Signage and user workflow
  • Uptime reporting and support

FAQCommon questions.

Still have questions?

Tell us about your site and our team will get back to you with clear next steps.

01Do you only supply chargers, or install them too?

Both. We can supply chargers alone, supply and install, or deliver the complete site including civil and electrical works.

02What happens during a site assessment?

We check the available power and load, the parking layout, cable routes and the civil and electrical scope, then recommend chargers and a phased plan.

03Who handles DISCOM approvals and transformer upgrades?

We include them in the survey and state them separately in the quote, so you can see what the charger costs and what the site needs.

04Can we start small and add chargers later?

Yes. We design the electrical scope with expansion in mind, so later chargers don't mean starting over.

05What support do you provide after launch?

Preventive maintenance, fault triage and service escalation, with AMC and managed-operations options. Service levels are agreed in the contract.
