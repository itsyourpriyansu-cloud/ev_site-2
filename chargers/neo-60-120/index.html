OverviewStart at 60 kW, upgrade to 120 kW without replacing the charger.

One floor-standing cabinet, 1100 × 2000 × 850 mm, with everything a driver and an operator need on the front panel.

  1. 01

    10.1-inch touch display

    LCD touch panel showing both guns side by side. The touchscreen is rated IK08.

  2. 02

    RFID, QR and app start

    Tap an RFID card (ISO/IEC 14443 A/B, FeliCa, MIFARE), scan the panel QR code or use the app with OTP. Auto Charge starts registered vehicles on plug-in.

  3. 03

    Emergency stop

    One emergency stop button on the front panel, backed by nine electrical protections.

  4. 04

    Two CCS2 guns

    CCS2 (Combo-2) at 150–1000 V DC, 250 A per gun, each output isolated from the other.

  5. 05

    5.5 m charging cables

    Usable cable length on each gun, so one cabinet serves two bays.

  6. 06

    Outdoor enclosure

    GI 120 GSM body with a fire-resistant polyester coat. IP55, IK10, rated −30 °C to +55 °C.

Key featuresBuilt into every unit.

01

60 kW now, 120 kW later

Ships with two 30 kW power modules. Add two more on site to reach 120 kW, with no new charger or footprint.

02

Dynamic load switching

Power is shared between the two guns module by module, so both bays stay productive.

03

OCPP 1.6J, 2.x-ready

Connects to your CMS over Wi-Fi, Ethernet or 4G, keeps working offline, and upgrades to OCPP 2.x.

04

IP55, IK10, outdoors

Rated from −30 °C to +55 °C, with nine electrical protections and an emergency stop on every unit.

FAQAbout the NEO Smart Series 60/120 kW.

Still have questions?

Tell us about your site and our team will get back to you with clear next steps.

01Can I start at 60 kW and upgrade later?

Yes. The base unit has two 30 kW modules. Two more can be added in the field to reach 120 kW, inside the same cabinet.

02Can both guns charge at the same time?

Yes. Power is shared between the two guns in module steps, so two vehicles charge at once and power moves to whichever needs it.

03Which vehicles can it charge?

Vehicles with a CCS2 (Combo-2) inlet, which most new electric cars in India use, from 150 to 1000 V DC. Compatibility for buses and commercial vehicles is confirmed per model.
