Partnership modelsChoose the model that fits you.

Match the commercial model to your capabilities, capital and customer relationships.

Model A

Authorised Distributor

You bring
Local sales, demo stock and working capital.
You earn
Product margin, installation/project margin and service referrals.
Best for
Established electrical, solar, auto or infrastructure dealers.

Model B

Turnkey Delivery Partner

You bring
Local execution of civil and electrical work, installation and commissioning, on an Envizon-led design.
You earn
Agreed project delivery margin and maintenance income.
Best for
Electrical contractors and EPC firms.

Model C

Site Operator / Revenue Share

You bring
A site, capex or local operations.
You earn
A share of charging revenue, on ownership, tariff, energy-cost and uptime terms defined up front.
Best for
Parking, retail, hospitality, fleets and property owners.

Optional model D

Sole operator / managed CPO.

  • Envizon EV or a designated operating entity manages agreed day-to-day operations, customer support, maintenance coordination and performance reporting.
  • The commercial structure can be a fixed management fee, a revenue share or a hybrid. Exclusivity and territory depend on the contract.

How we priceThree prices, so the scope is always clear.

Equipment, project delivery and recurring services are always quoted separately.

1

Equipment price

Charger model + power rating + connector/cable + connectivity + optional pedestal or protection.

2

Project price

Equipment + design + installation + civil/electrical works + utility upgrades + commissioning + taxes.

3

Recurring price

AMC / preventive maintenance + remote support + CMS/software + payment fees + site operations, where applicable.

A charger-only price is never presented as a turnkey price. Each quote states warranty start, freight, GST, payment milestones, lead time and validity.

Site partnershipsWho brings what, and how revenue is shared.

The site partner contributes

Land or parking bays, access, customer footfall and possibly part of the capex. Rent, power supply, access hours, signage and exclusivity are agreed up front.

Envizon EV contributes

Charger selection, project delivery, agreed software and operations, maintenance coordination and reporting, only as contracted.

Revenue waterfallFrom receipts to payouts.

  1. 01

    Charging receipts

    Everything drivers pay for charging at the site.

  2. 02

    Less agreed deductions

    Shared on the agreed split and settlement schedule.

Agreed up front

Commercial terms we lock down.

  • The revenue split and what it applies to: gross receipts, net receipts, or profit after defined costs.
  • Asset ownership, capex recovery, tariff control, billing and settlement frequency, audit rights and reporting.
  • Uptime and service levels, fault response, insurance, safety, customer complaints, termination and asset transfer.

FAQPartner questions.

Still have questions?

Tell us about your site and our team will get back to you with clear next steps.

01Who can become a partner?

Electrical, solar, automotive and infrastructure businesses, EPC contractors, property and parking owners, and fleet operators. The right model depends on what you already do.

02How much do I need to invest?

It depends on the model. Distributors invest in demo stock and working capital; site partners may contribute a site or capex. Illustrative economics are in the partner pack, shared after the first conversation.

03Is my territory exclusive?

Exclusivity and territory are agreed in the contract, based on the model and the plan.

04Do you train our technicians?

Yes. Onboarding includes product training and certification for partner technicians, plus survey and commissioning checklists.

05Do I need to hold stock?

Distributors keep demo stock. Delivery partners and site operators usually don't.

06How is revenue share settled?

On the split, deductions and settlement frequency agreed in the contract, with reporting and audit rights.
