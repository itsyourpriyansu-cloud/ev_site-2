Key featuresBuilt into every unit.

60 kW now, 120 kW later

Ships with two 30 kW power modules. Add two more on site to reach 120 kW, with no new charger or footprint.

Dynamic load switching

Power is shared between the two guns module by module, so both bays stay productive.

FAQAbout the NEO Smart 60/120.

Still have questions?

Tell us about your site and our team will get back to you with clear next steps.

01Can I start at 60 kW and upgrade later?

Yes. The base unit has two 30 kW modules. Two more can be added in the field to reach 120 kW, inside the same cabinet.

02Can both guns charge at the same time?

Yes. Power is shared between the two guns in module steps, so two vehicles charge at once and power moves to whichever needs it.

03Which vehicles can it charge?

Vehicles with a CCS2 (Combo-2) inlet, which most new electric cars in India use, from 150 to 1000 V DC. Compatibility for buses and commercial vehicles is confirmed per model.
